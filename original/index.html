The Problem
Running a print farm means staff. Always.
Every finished print sits on the bed until someone removes it. That someone works in shifts, around the clock.
👥

Staff in 2–3 shifts

Someone has to be there to pull parts and restart prints — day and night.

⏱️

Up to 30% idle time

Printers sit idle between print end and operator arrival. That's capacity wasted.

📈

Scaling costs grow linearly

More printers means more people. There's no way to grow without growing headcount.

EMA removes the operator from the loop entirely. Printers run, cool, eject, and restart on their own — 24/7, without anyone in the room.
How It Works
Five steps. Fully autonomous.
Each automation cycle runs autonomously — from cooling to ejection to the next print start.
01

Print completes

The printer finishes the job and signals the system.

02

Rapid cooling

Four blowers cool the part and bed simultaneously.

03

Bed flexes

A push mechanism bends the bed, detaching the part.

04

Part ejected

The printhead attachment sweeps the part off the bed.

05

Next cycle starts

The printer automatically begins the next print. Repeat indefinitely.

The Product
EMA — Hardware + Software, ready to deploy
The EMA installs on your existing printer with no modifications. The web service handles file preparation — no G-code knowledge required.
📦

Compact form factor

Adds only ~15 cm in height. Width and depth stay the same — ideal for dense farm setups.

♾️

Unlimited print cycles

Works with a single bed — no extra consumables or bed replacements needed.

⚡

Fast cooling

4 blowers with optimized nozzle geometry significantly cut inter-cycle wait time vs. passive cooling.

🔧

Fully configurable

Each stage tunable: cooling temperature, timings, eject height, bed cleaning mode.

🔌

Powered by the printer

No extra outlets or wiring — the EMA draws power directly from the printer.
